What We Offer

Comprehensive Healthcare Services

From everyday free consultations and examination to specialized community programs, we provide a wide range of services designed to meet the needs of the people of Buyende District.

Immunization

Free immunisation services providing safe, approved vaccines to protect children and adults from preventable diseases.

Family planning

Compassionate family planning services, education, and support for individuals and families.

pediatrics

Compassionate healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents

Laboratory

Quality laboratory services helping vulnerable families access better healthcare.

Antenatal care

Antenatal care services providing compassionate pregnancy support, regular checkups, and guidance for mothers in a safe and welcoming environment.

In-patient

In-patient services providing quality care, monitoring, and treatment for patients in a comfortable and supportive environment.

Health Education

Health education services providing communities with knowledge, awareness, and guidance on disease prevention, healthy living, and improved wellbeing.

HIV Counseling and Test

Free HIV counseling and testing services providing confidential support, accurate information, and early detection to promote prevention, treatment, and healthier communities.

Free safe male circumcision

Free circumcision services providing safe, professional, and confidential procedures to promote health, hygiene, and disease prevention within the community.

Care That Reaches Everyone

At Maliya Foundation Healthcare Center, we believe no one should be denied healthcare because of poverty. We provide free medical services to vulnerable individuals and families, bringing hope, healing, and compassionate care to the communities we serve..
