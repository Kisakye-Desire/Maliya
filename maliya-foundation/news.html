Speaker Anita Among Donates Shs50 Million Toward Maternity Ward

Speaker of Parliament, Rt. Hon. Anita Among, has donated Shs50 million toward the completion of a maternity ward at Maliya Foundation Health Care Centre in Kagulu-Igwaya Trading Centre, Buyende District. The contribution was handed over to Prossy Mpindi, Founder of Maliya Foundation Health Care Centre, to support the completion of the maternity ward and strengthen access to maternal healthcare for women in the community. Accompanied by Hon. Moses Magogo, Member of Parliament for Budiope East, the Speaker commended Maliya Foundation for providing affordable healthcare services

The maternity ward will help address the shortage of maternal healthcare services in the area and provide mothers with safer and more accessible care closer to home. Maliya Foundation Health Care Centre appreciates the Rt. Hon. Speaker for this generous contribution and calls upon other partners, well-wishers and stakeholders to join efforts to improve healthcare services in Buyende.

Maliya Foundation Health Care Centre Takes Free Healthcare Closer to Communities

Maliya Foundation Health Care Centre continues to bring essential healthcare services closer to vulnerable families through its community health outreach programs across Buyende and surrounding communities. Through these outreaches, our healthcare team provides free medical consultations, health screenings, basic treatment, health education and disease prevention support to people who may face challenges accessing healthcare services. The outreach activities also focus on raising awareness about preventive healthcare, maternal and child health, hygiene, common diseases and the importance of seeking medical attention early.

By taking healthcare services directly into communities, Maliya Foundation Health Care Centre is helping identify health concerns early and connect people with appropriate care and support. These community outreaches are part of our continued commitment to ensuring that vulnerable families can access quality healthcare regardless of their financial circumstances.
