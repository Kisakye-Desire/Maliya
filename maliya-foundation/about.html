Our Story

A Dream Born Out of Love, Compassion, and Hope

Maliya Foundation Healthcare Center was founded in November 2021 by Mrs. Mpindi Prossy in loving memory of her mother, the late Mrs. Maliya Naudo. Inspired by her mother's compassion and kindness, Prossy established the healthcare center to ensure that vulnerable people in Kagulu could access quality healthcare with dignity and hope.

Having witnessed the challenges many families faced in obtaining medical care, she turned her vision into reality by creating a community-centered health facility dedicated to serving mothers, children, the elderly, and other vulnerable groups. Since its founding, the center has provided essential healthcare services, health education, and compassionate care to those in need.

Although the center continues to face challenges in expanding its services, it remains committed to improving lives through the support of partners, donors, and well-wishers. Today, Maliya Foundation Healthcare Center proudly carries forward the legacy of Mrs. Maliya Naudo by bringing hope, healing, and quality healthcare closer to the community.

Together, we believe that a healthier community is a stronger community.
Background

Why We Were Established

Buyende District is one of Uganda's most underserved regions when it comes to healthcare. Long distances to the nearest hospital, shortages of medical supplies and high costs put essential care out of reach for many families.

Maliya Foundation was established to close this gap to bring healthcare closer to the people, to treat the most vulnerable with dignity, and to build a healthier future for the entire community.

Challenges We Face

As we strive to provide quality healthcare to every patient, we continue to face several challenges that limit our ability to meet the growing demand for medical services. Your support can help us overcome these barriers and improve the lives of those we serve.

Help Us Make a Difference

Shortage of Essential Medicines

Many patients cannot receive the medications they need due to limited drug supplies. Your donations help us keep life-saving medicines in stock.

Limited Medical Equipment

We need modern diagnostic and treatment equipment to provide timely, accurate, and quality healthcare services.

Limited Space for Patient Care

The growing number of patients we serve has outgrown our available space, leading to overcrowding and longer waiting times. Support for expanding our facilities will help us provide more comfortable and efficient healthcare services.

How You Can Help

By donating medicines, medical equipment, financial support, or partnering with us, you help us provide better healthcare and reach more people in need.
