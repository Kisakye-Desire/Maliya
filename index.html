Welcome to Maliya Foundation

A Healing Hand for the Community

Maliya Foundation Health Care Center is a non-profit healthcare facility located in Kagulu, Buyende District, dedicated to providing compassionate and affordable healthcare to vulnerable communities.

Since 2021, we have been a source of hope and healing through free medical care, maternal and child health services, health education, and disease prevention programs. With the support of donors and partners, we continue to reach more families and restore lives through love, care, and service.

Speaker Anita Among Donates Shs50 Million Towards Maternity Ward

Speaker of Parliament, Rt. Hon. Anita Among, has donated Shs50 million toward the completion of a maternity ward at Maliya Foundation Health Care Centre in Kagulu-Igwaya Trading Centre, Buyende District.

The contribution was handed over to Mrs Prossy Mpindi, Founder of Maliya Foundation Health Care Centre, to support the completion of the maternity ward and strengthen access to maternal healthcare for women in the community.
